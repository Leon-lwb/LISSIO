## Supported sources

- Current releases published through the Codeberg releases page.
- The active default development branch.

Historical releases are not maintained separately. Security-supported versions
are described in `SECURITY.md`.

## Getting help

Use the repository issue tracker for reproducible bugs, feature proposals,
documentation corrections, and general usage questions. Include the Lissio
version, operating system, connection or protocol involved, reproduction steps,
and non-sensitive logs.

Use `SECURITY.md` for vulnerabilities, intellectual-property concerns, and
trademark reports. Do not post secrets or exploitable details publicly.

## What support includes

Maintainers may clarify documented behavior, investigate reproducible defects,
review compatibility reports, and explain contribution requirements.

## What support does not include

The project does not provide guaranteed response times, consulting, custom
development, device-specific integration, legal advice, or support for modified
builds unless maintainers explicitly agree otherwise.

## Commercial use

Apache-2.0 permits commercial use, modification, and redistribution subject to
its license and notice conditions. Lissio does not sell a separate proprietary
license or support contract.
